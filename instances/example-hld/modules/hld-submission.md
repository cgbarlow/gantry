## Purpose statement

This document proposes a self-service Provider Portal for GPs and nurse practitioners to submit Disability Allowance certificates directly into EOS, for approval by the Technical Architecture Committee.

## Decision requested

Approve. TAC is asked to approve the proposed Provider Portal solution so the initiative can proceed into Detailed Design.

## Consultation

Consulted: Architecture Council (endorsed, no dissent); EOS platform team (endorsed the integration approach); Contoso Information Security Advisor (raised no objection to the identity approach, pending fuller security detail at Build Readiness).

## Next steps

On approval, proceeds to Detailed Design (SAD/SSAD) and then Design Committee for build-readiness sign-off ahead of Build Readiness Confirmed.
