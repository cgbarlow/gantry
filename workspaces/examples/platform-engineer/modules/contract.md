# Contract

## Terms

Permanent employment agreement on Contoso's standard terms for Technology Band 4, drawn 1 April 2026.

The agreement reflects the offer as accepted, including the revised start date of 20 April. One difference from the offer: the on-call allowance is expressed in the agreement as a schedule reference rather than a figure, which is standard and was explained to the candidate before signing.

The executed agreement is held by HR. It is not attached to this instance.

## Signatures

Hiring manager signed 2 April 2026 (Tomas Berg) — one day after the contract was drawn.

Candidate returned her electronic signature 3 April 2026, the day after it was issued.

Both signatures were faster than the process assumes. Nothing in the delay that followed was caused by the contract.

## Start date

20 April 2026, as agreed during the offer negotiation. The original proposed date was 13 April.

The date did not move after the contract was signed, though it came close — see the payroll rework.

## Elapsed time

Nineteen days from contract creation on 1 April to start date on 20 April, against the fifteen the process assumes.

The four extra days sit entirely in payroll. Contract creation to candidate signature took two days against the assumed four to eight, so the front of the sequence was ahead of schedule; the payroll details request and its failed validation then consumed seven days against the assumed four.

Put plainly: this hire was running early until the payroll step, finished four days late, and the whole of the difference is one email that was not seen for five days.
