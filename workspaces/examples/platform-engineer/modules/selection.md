# Selection

## Shortlist

Four candidates shortlisted from thirty-one applications, on criteria agreed with the panel before the role was advertised and published in the advertisement:

- Demonstrated ownership of a container platform used by other teams, not only consumed by them
- Evidence of reducing operational load rather than absorbing it
- Ability to carry a production on-call rotation
- Written communication — the role owns documentation, and this was assessed from the application itself

All four met the first three. The fourth criterion separated them: two wrote clearly about work they had done and two described work in terms that did not survive a follow-up question.

## Interviews

Two rounds, 10 to 17 March 2026.

Round one, 10 to 12 March: a one-hour conversation with Tomas Berg (Platform Engineering Lead) and Mereana Walker (Platform Engineer, incumbent), covering the candidate's platform experience and a walkthrough of a deployment path they had built.

Round two, 16 to 17 March: ninety minutes with Tomas Berg, Sam Okafor (Head of Information Security) and Hana Te Rangi (HR Business Partner). A working session on a real problem from the team's queue — making a database credential rotation unattended — followed by a discussion of the on-call rotation and how the candidate had handled a production incident.

Three of the four shortlisted attended both rounds; one withdrew after round one having accepted another offer.

## Preferred candidate

Marama Clarke, selected 18 March 2026.

Against the criteria: Marama has spent four years on a platform team of comparable size, and the work she walked through in round one was the self-service half of that platform rather than its infrastructure — the closest match in the field to what this role is actually for. In round two she took the credential rotation problem apart in a way the panel had not considered, and was explicit about what she did not know.

The panel's reservation, recorded because it was genuine: she has not previously worked somewhere with a formal change process, and named that herself as the part of the role she expected to find hardest. The panel judged this a ramp rather than a risk, and it is reflected in the first-week plan.

Panel recommendation was unanimous.

## Unsuccessful candidates

The two candidates who completed both rounds were told by telephone on 19 March, the day after the decision, by Hana Te Rangi, and both were offered feedback. One accepted and had a twenty-minute call on 23 March.

The candidate who withdrew after round one was acknowledged the same day.

The twenty-seven applicants not shortlisted were notified by 9 March, within three days of the advertisement closing.
