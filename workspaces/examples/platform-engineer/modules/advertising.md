# Advertising

## Channels

- Contoso careers site
- Seek
- LinkedIn — paid listing, three weeks
- Internal vacancies list and the Technology all-hands channel
- Two platform engineering community Slack workspaces, posted by Tomas Berg

## Advertising period

Open 13 February 2026, closed 6 March 2026 — three weeks.

Not extended and not re-run. The LinkedIn listing was refreshed once on 24 February, which is a paid re-promotion rather than an extension; the closing date did not move.

## Response

Thirty-one applications received.

Fit was better than the team expected. Eleven were assessed as meeting the core requirements, of which six had directly comparable container platform experience and five were adjacent — infrastructure or SRE backgrounds that would have needed a longer ramp. The remaining twenty were not close: most were general software engineering applications with no platform experience, and four were clearly untargeted.

The community Slack posts produced four applications, three of which made the eleven. That is a much better hit rate than any other channel and is worth repeating.

## Notes

The internal vacancies list produced no applications, which is consistent with the last two Technology vacancies. Worth asking whether it is doing anything.

Agency channels were not used and were not needed at this band. The 2025 Senior Platform Engineer role did use an agency; the difference in response quality between the two does not obviously justify the fee.
