# Glossary

## Terms and definitions

| Preferred term | Definition | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approved to Recruit | The confirmation, given by signing off Requisition, that the role may go to market. It follows the approval chain's decision and role evaluation; it is not the chain's own decision. | Requisition approved, headcount approval |
| Appointment Case | The case put to executive approval to appoint the preferred candidate, before any offer is made. | Offer approval, hire approval |
| Engagement type | How the person will be engaged (for example Permanent). It fixes the approval route the requisition follows. | Contract type, employment category |
| Hire Record | The complete record of the hire, in process order, kept as the file for the hire. | Personnel file, onboarding file |
| Manager Handover | The note sent to the hiring manager once the starter's account exists, saying what they must do before the start date. | Manager checklist, welcome email |
| Non-standard hardware | A device other than the standard Technology issue, which needs a decision from the hiring manager. | Special device, exception laptop |
| Role evaluation | Placing the role in a pay band by comparing it with existing roles, confirmed by HR against the remuneration framework. | Job sizing, grading |
| Starter | The person who has accepted the offer and is being set up to work on their first day. | New joiner, new hire |
| Vetting | The pre-employment checks (right to work, criminal record, referees, identity) run on the preferred candidate. | Background check, screening |
