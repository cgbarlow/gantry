# Background and context

## Problem statement

Kiwi Cover Mutual (KCM) cannot settle a straightforward home or motor claim in under twelve working days, and every claim touches at least four separately operated systems.

Claims handling still runs on the platform inherited from the 2014 ArchiSurance merger. Policy data and the claims ledger sit in Policy Data Management on the mainframe, customer contact in the CRM System, payments are keyed by hand into the Home & Away Financial Application, and scanned documents sit in a document management system the others can't read. A handler re-keys each claim across all four. Median time to payment is 12.4 working days against a member expectation of five, around 18 percent of claims are re-keyed at least once, and only two contractors still hold mainframe skills.

## Affected domains

- Claims handling (register, accept, valuate, pay)
- Policy administration
- Customer contact and CRM
- Finance and payments
- Document management
- Technology operations

## Opportunity

A member reports a claim once, through the channel of their choice, and KCM handles it as one Handle Claim process backed by shared customer, policy and claim data, paying the member the day the claim is approved.

Policy Data Management stays the system of record during the transition, exposed through services rather than re-keying, so the mainframe can be retired on its own timetable.

## Success criteria

- Median notification-to-payment time for standard claims at or below five working days within two quarters of go-live.
- Claim re-key rate below three percent.
- Ninety-five percent of approved claims paid within one working day.
- No increase in the fraud referral rate and no loss of audit trail, verified at the first post-go-live audit.
