Gantry

Every organisation runs on documents. Most processes ask for the same information again at every sign-off, in a slightly different form.

With Gantry you write it once. At each sign-off it prepares the document that sign-off asks for, from the same information.

    Note 1

    Start with what you have

    Give Gantry the documents you already have: the brief, the emails, the meeting notes, last time's version. An AI reads them, drafts each step, and shows which document every fact came from.

    Note 2

    You stay in charge

    Where your documents leave a gap or disagree with each other, it asks you. Nothing is saved until you say so, and the sign-off stays with a person.

    Note 3

    Word comes out

    Each sign-off gets its document as a Word file. Reviewers comment in Word the way they always have, and Gantry works through their comments with you.

    Service, implementation or development
